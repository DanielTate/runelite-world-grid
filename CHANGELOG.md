# Changelog

## 1.1

- A redesigned side panel.
- **You are in** shows the square you're standing in, updating as you move.
- Every setting is now in the side panel too: switches to show or hide the grid in game, on
  the world map and in the reference box, plus the square size, grid colour and line
  thickness.
- Saved locations are easier to read, with Pin, Map and Delete as simple links under
  each place.
- A new icon.
